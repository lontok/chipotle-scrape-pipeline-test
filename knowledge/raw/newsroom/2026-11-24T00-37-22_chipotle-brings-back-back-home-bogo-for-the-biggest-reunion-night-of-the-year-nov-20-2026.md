**The Kickoff to Thanksgiving Weekend**

The night before Thanksgiving has become an unofficial hometown reunion, as friends return for the holiday and make plans to reconnect. Dining out plays a central role in gatherings, with 76% of Gen Z consumers saying they go out to restaurants to socialize with friends and family, according to Morning Consult.3

The Back Home BOGO debuted in 2025 and became Chipotle's largest sales-driving campaign of the year.

"Thanksgiving Eve is when the hometown group chat comes to life," **said Stephanie Perdue, Senior Vice President of Brand Marketing at Chipotle.** "Whether Chipotle is the first stop of the night or where the crew ends up later, we're giving friends a place to get together over real food. The response last year showed us how much our guests embraced the occasion, and we're excited to bring it back as another reason for hometown crews to reconnect before Thanksgiving."

**Cyber Weekend $0 Delivery Fee Offer**

After Thanksgiving Eve reunions and Thanksgiving Day celebrations, Chipotle is inviting fans to give the leftovers a break. From Friday, November 27 through Monday, November 30, guests can enjoy a $0 delivery fee2 on orders placed through the Chipotle app, Chipotle.com, and Chipotle.ca with code CYBER26.

1 – BOGO exclusive to Chipotle Rewards members who scan for Rewards at point of sale at time of purchase. Offer is limited to five free entrée items per check and is subject to availability. Each free entrée requires purchase of an entrée item of equal or greater value. Valid only on November 25, 2026, 5 p.m. to 9 p.m. local time. Redeemable in-restaurant only, at participating U.S. Chipotle locations; not valid for catering, Burritos by the Box, mobile, online or delivery orders, or orders placed via third-party platforms. Kids' meals do not count as an entrée purchase. Purchased entrées are eligible for Chipotle Rewards points; promotion may not otherwise be combined with other coupons, promotions, or special offers. Additional restrictions may apply; void where prohibited.

2 – Higher menu prices are charged for delivery; additional service and other fees are applied at checkout as well (except in CA, CO, CT, MA, MN and VA). Available only November 27 through November 30, 2026, within Chipotle's delivery areas from participating U.S. and Canada locations, during normal operating hours for such locations. Use of promo code CYBER26 at time of order is required. Minimum order $10 USD / $12 CAD; maximum item numbers apply as well, each excluding tax and fees. Deliveries and redemptions are subject to availability. Offer is not valid on catering or Burritos by the Box orders. Redemptions of Chipotle Rewards and other promotional offers may be included in a qualifying delivery order but do not count toward satisfaction of minimum purchase requirements. Valid only on Chipotle website or the Chipotle app; not valid on orders placed via third-party delivery platforms. Chipotle reserves the right to modify or terminate this offer at any time without notice. Additional restrictions may apply; void where prohibited.

**When is Chipotle's Back Home BOGO in 2026 and what is Chipotle offering?** Chipotle's Back Home BOGO returns on Thanksgiving Eve, Wednesday, November 25, 2026. From 5 to 9 p.m. local time, Chipotle Rewards members can receive a free entrée with the purchase of an entrée at participating U.S. restaurants.

**How is Chipotle celebrating Thanksgiving Eve in 2026?** Chipotle is bringing back its popular Back Home BOGO, giving Rewards members a BUY-ONE-GET-ONE (BOGO) entrée offer in-restaurant on Thanksgiving Eve.

**How do I get Chipotle's Back Home BOGO on Thanksgiving Eve?** To redeem the offer, Chipotle Rewards members must purchase an entrée in-restaurant and scan for Rewards at checkout to receive a second entrée for free.

**Where can I redeem Chipotle's Back Home BOGO?** The Back Home BOGO is available in-restaurant at participating Chipotle locations in the U.S. The offer is not valid on mobile, online, delivery or third-party orders.

**What is Chipotle offering for Cyber Weekend 2026?** From November 27 through November 30, 2026, Chipotle is offering a $0 delivery fee on qualifying orders from participating U.S. and Canada locations with code CYBER26.
