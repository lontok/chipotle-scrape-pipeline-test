NEWPORT BEACH, Calif., Nov. 16, 2026 / [PRNewswireOpens in new window](http://www.prnewswire.com/)/ \-\- Forget popcorn. With two of the year's biggest movie releases set to hit theaters in the weeks ahead, Chipotle Mexican Grill (NYSE: CMG) today announced a new way for fans to bring their favorite entrée to their seats. Introducing the "Sneak It In" hoodie, a limited-edition sweatshirt with a built-in insulated pocket designed to stash a Chipotle burrito, bowl, salad or tacos and help keep it warm on the way to the theater.

**Built for Burritos. Made for the Movies.**

The "Sneak It In" hoodie has everything a Chipotle fan needs to turn dinner and a movie into dinner at the movie:

- **A discreet burrito pocket:** Hidden inside the hoodie, a lined and insulated pocket is sized to fit a Chipotle burrito, bowl, salad or tacos and help keep it warm until showtime.
- **A dedicated napkin pocket:** Because bringing the burrito but forgetting the napkins is a rookie move.
- **A streetwear-inspired fit:** Designed with an oversized, unisex fit for a relaxed streetwear look.
- **Three colorways:** The "Sneak It In" hoodie will be available in three colorways:
  - Nothing To See Here Black
  - No Burrito Here Brown
  - Got the Goods Gray

The limited-edition "Sneak It In" hoodie will retail for $53 USD with free shipping and will be available exclusively at [ChipotleGoods.comOpens in new window](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4819752-1&h=3941236944&u=https%3A%2F%2Fchipotlegoods.com%2F&a=%C2%A0ChipotleGoods.com) in the U.S. and Canada beginning November 18 at 9 a.m. PT, while supplies last. Each hoodie comes with a free entrée card to put that burrito pocket to good use at the movies or wherever fans want to enjoy Chipotle.2

Chipotle Rewards members in the U.S. can enter to win a "Sneak It In" hoodie through the Chipotle Rewards Exchange. From November 16 through November 22, members can exchange 10 points to enter the sweepstakes, with a maximum of 20 entries.3

"Everything about going to the movies has gotten better over the years. Bigger screens, better sound, more comfortable seats. But the food? We're still choosing between popcorn, candy and nachos," said **Kirby Ann Connell, Senior Director of Social and Influencers at Chipotle**. "Movie nights deserve real, craveable food. And since our fans were already sneaking Chipotle to theaters, we figured we'd make it a little easier. Sometimes innovation is as simple as putting a burrito pocket in a hoodie."
