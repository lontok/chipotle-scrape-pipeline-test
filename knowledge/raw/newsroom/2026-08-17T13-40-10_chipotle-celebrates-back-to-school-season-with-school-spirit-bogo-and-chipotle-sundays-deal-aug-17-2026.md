NEWPORT BEACH, Calif., Aug. 17, 2026 / [PRNewswireOpens in new window](http://www.prnewswire.com/)/ \-\- Chipotle Mexican Grill (NYSE: CMG) today announced a School Spirit BOGO and three consecutive weeks of a "Chipotle Sundays" dinner offer, bringing more value to fans during the back-to-school season.

**Chipotle School Spirit BOGO** As students head back to the classroom, Chipotle is giving fans another reason to rep their pride with a School Spirit BOGO. On Thursday, August 20, guests wearing school apparel or accessories can purchase an entrée at participating Chipotle restaurants in the U.S. from 3 p.m. to close local time and receive a second entrée for free. From middle and high school spirit wear to college tees, sweatshirts, jerseys and hats, school gear is the ticket to a free entrée.1
